Our Legacy

At B.U. Bhandari, we are riders and engineers.

A part of the B.U. Bhandari Group — a trusted Pune business house since 1920 — our Honda dealership has been riding with the city since 2002. We've served more than One Lakh Happy Families, guided by a simple belief: every customer is part of our family.

Whether you're buying your first scooter or your dream adventure bike, our team treats your journey with the same care we'd give our own rides — honest advice, genuine parts and service that keeps you moving.
